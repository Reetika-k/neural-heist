How to play

THE JOB

Each mission: train one AI teammate in the Lab, then use it to steal the data drive and reach the exit. Your teammate performs exactly as well as you trained it.

↑↓←→ (or WASD)Move
ShiftSneak: slower, and guards notice you more slowly
EUse the vault console (Mission 2) or the terminal (Mission 3)
Tab or 🧠AI Brain panel: every AI's live numbers, and export
M or 🔊Mute
EscMenu · Enter restart after failing
  • Guards have vision cones. White → yellow → red as they grow suspicious. A full meter means a chase; contact means caught. Walls block sight.
  • Alarm: the first chase (or a laser, or 3 wrong passwords) starts a 25-second alarm that never resets.
  • Stars: finish · never chased · under par time. Score adds your AI's accuracy.
